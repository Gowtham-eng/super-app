RefexOne Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how RefexOne collects, uses, and protects personal information when you use the RefexOne website and mobile applications.

Effective date: 12 August 2026
Controller: REFEX HOLDING PRIVATE LIMITED and its group companies (“Refex”, “we”, “us”)

1. About RefexOne

RefexOne is an employee workspace that lets authorised Refex Group users sign in and open assigned business applications (for example HR, expense, workflow, facility, and IT support tools) from a single launcher on the web and on iOS/Android.

Service URL: https://refexone.com

2. Who this policy applies to

This policy applies to employees, contractors, and other authorised users who access RefexOne with a company account. RefexOne is not intended for the general public or for children.

3. Information we collect

Depending on how you sign in and use the service, we may process:

  • Account and contact details — such as name, work email address, and user ID
  • Authentication data — credentials you enter, or identity assertions from Microsoft Entra ID / Azure AD or other SSO providers configured by your organisation
  • Session data — tokens or cookies needed to keep you signed in and authorise app access
  • Usage and access records — such as login events, app launches, and admin audit logs where enabled for security and compliance
  • Support content — information you submit in IT or access requests
  • Device/browser technical data — limited technical information required to operate the web or mobile app (for example browser type or basic device context used by the session)

We do not use RefexOne for advertising. The native mobile apps do not include third-party advertising SDKs.

4. How we use your information

We use personal data to:

  • Authenticate you and provide the RefexOne launcher and assigned apps
  • Enable single sign-on into applications you are authorised to use
  • Manage user access, roles, groups, and admin settings
  • Provide IT support and process access or service requests
  • Maintain security, prevent misuse, and meet legal or audit obligations
  • Improve reliability and support of the service

Legal bases typically include performance of an employment/contractor relationship, legitimate interests in securing corporate systems, and compliance with applicable law.

5. App Store / Play Store privacy summary

For the RefexOne mobile apps, data commonly associated with your account includes:

  • Email address
  • Name
  • User ID

These are used for App Functionality, are linked to your identity, and are not used for tracking across other companies’ apps or websites for advertising.

6. Sharing of information

We may share personal data with:

  • Refex group companies, as needed to operate employee systems
  • IT and identity service providers that host or support RefexOne (under contract)
  • Business applications you open through RefexOne (for example SSO to those apps), which then process data under their own policies and your organisation’s access rules
  • Authorities or other parties when required by law or to protect rights and security

We do not sell personal information.

7. Linked applications and third-party services

When you launch an app from RefexOne, that application may collect and process additional information under its own privacy terms. Please review those apps’ policies where applicable. Corporate website practices for Refex Industries Limited are also described at https://www.refex.co.in/privacy-policy.

8. Cookies and local storage

RefexOne uses cookies and browser/app storage (such as local session tokens) that are necessary to keep you signed in, route SSO flows, and operate the service. These are not used for cross-app advertising tracking.

9. Security

We apply administrative and technical measures appropriate for an enterprise identity and app-access platform, including access controls and encrypted transport (HTTPS). No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

10. Retention

We retain account and access-related data for as long as your account is active and as needed for security, audit, legal, and operational purposes. When access is removed, data may be deleted or retained in backups/archives as required by policy or law.

11. Your rights and choices

Subject to applicable law and company policy, you may request access, correction, or deletion of personal data, or raise a privacy concern, by contacting your Refex IT / HR administrator or the contact below. Because RefexOne is a workplace system, some requests may be handled through your employer.

12. Children’s privacy

RefexOne is a workplace application and is not directed to children under 16 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

13. International processing

RefexOne may be hosted or supported using infrastructure in India or other locations used by Refex and its service providers. Where data is transferred, we take steps consistent with applicable law.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised effective date. Continued use of RefexOne after an update means you acknowledge the revised policy.
